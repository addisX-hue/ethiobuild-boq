Last updated: 5 November 2026 · Addis Engineering

1. About these terms

AddisBOQ (addisboq.com) and its tools — the BOQ calculator, Site Log and Formwork Optimizer — are run by Addis Engineering in Ethiopia. By using the site you agree to these terms. If you do not agree, please do not use the site.

2. How estimates are produced

AddisBOQ produces detailed, component-based cost estimates using engineering ratios developed by a structural engineer and applied to the parameters you enter. They are built for budgeting and early planning. Final quantities depend on your drawings, soil, site conditions and contractor, so they should be confirmed by a licensed engineer before tendering or construction.

You are responsible for how you use an estimate.

3. Rates and prices

Unit rates reflect Ethiopian market prices as of the date shown on the site, adjusted for the selected city. Construction prices change often. Always confirm prices with local suppliers and contractors before buying materials or signing contracts.

4. Paid exports (Excel and PDF)

Calculating a BOQ is free. The itemized Excel or PDF export is a one-time payment made by TeleBirr, at the price shown in the payment window.

  • A payment unlocks the BOQ currently on screen. If you change inputs and recalculate, the new BOQ needs a new payment.
  • Each TeleBirr transaction ID can be used once.
  • Save your file right after downloading. Files are generated in your browser and are not stored on our servers.
  • If your payment was verified but the download failed, contact us with your transaction ID within 7 days and we will help you get the file.
  • Payments are not refundable once a file has been generated, except if a server error stopped you from receiving your file, you were charged twice, or the law requires it. To request a refund, contact us within 7 days of payment by email, Telegram or phone, with your TeleBirr transaction ID.

5. Site Log

Site Log is free for the first 14 days. After that, access is extended in 30-day periods paid by TeleBirr at the price shown in the app. Access is tied to your project code, so keep it private: anyone with the code can see and edit that project. Unused days are not refunded. Export your records regularly; we cannot guarantee recovery of data that is lost or deleted.

6. Your data

  • BOQ calculator: building parameters (area, floors, materials, city) are sent to our server to calculate the BOQ. Project name, client name and phone number stay in your browser and are not sent.
  • Payments: your TeleBirr transaction ID is sent to a payment-verification service to confirm the payment, and kept so it cannot be reused.
  • Site Log: project records you enter are stored on our servers so you can access them.

We do not sell your data or use it for advertising.

BOQs and files you pay for are yours to use for your own projects and to share with your client, contractor or consultant. You may not copy, resell or automate the service, try to bypass payment, or overload or attack it.
